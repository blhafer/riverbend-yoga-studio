About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend is a small neighborhood studio built on one idea: yoga should feel calm, inclusive, and supportive for everyone who walks through the door.

Meet Our Instructors

Elena Brooks, Founder and Lead Instructor

Elena has taught yoga for 15 years and holds an E-RYT 500 certification. She opened Riverbend after years of teaching beginners who felt out of place in fast-paced classes. She leads Gentle Yoga and most private sessions.

Marcus Hale, Vinyasa Instructor

Marcus teaches the early and lunchtime flows. A former office manager, he designs short, focused classes for professionals who need to move and reset during the workday.

Grace Okafor, Restorative and Chair Yoga Instructor

Grace is certified in chair yoga and yoga for older adults. Her restorative classes focus on rest, balance, and joint-friendly movement.

Accessibility Accommodations

  • Step-free entrance and an accessible restroom
  • Chairs, blocks, straps, bolsters, and blankets in every class at no charge
  • Chair-based options for every pose in Gentle Yoga
  • Mats placed near the wall or a chair on request for extra stability
  • Large-print schedules available at the front desk
  • Instructors who give clear spoken cues, so you never have to watch to follow along

Let us know about any needs when you request information, and we will have your space ready.

Studio Policies

  • Arrival: Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first class to meet your instructor.
  • Late arrival: Doors close 5 minutes after class begins so the room stays calm.
  • Cancellations: Cancel at least 12 hours ahead to keep your class credit.
  • Mats: Bring your own or borrow a cleaned studio mat for free.
  • Phones: Please silence phones inside the practice room.
  • Fragrance-free: We ask everyone to skip strong scents out of respect for sensitive students.

Studio Hours
