Classes and Pricing

Every class is taught at a calm pace with options for each pose. Choose the style that fits your energy, schedule, and goals.

Class Types

Gentle Yoga

Slow, supported movement and stretching. Chair options are offered in every class, making it ideal for beginners and older adults.

60 minutes · All levels

Vinyasa Flow

Poses linked with the breath in a steady flow that builds strength and focus. Our 45-minute lunchtime and early classes fit a busy workday.

45 or 60 minutes · Some experience helpful

Restorative Yoga

Long, fully supported rests with bolsters and blankets to calm the nervous system and ease stress.

60 minutes · All levels

      • Personal plan with poses you can practice at home
      • Available with any of our instructors
