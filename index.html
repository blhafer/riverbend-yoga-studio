New to Yoga? You Belong Here

  • No experience needed

    Every class offers simple options, and instructors explain each pose step by step.

  • Go at your own pace

    Chairs, blocks, and bolsters are always available, and resting is always allowed.

  • Your first class is free

    Try any gentle or restorative class at no cost before you choose a pass.

Try a Short Guided Breathing Practice

Not sure where to start? Press play, sit comfortably, and follow along with one of our instructors.

Transcript summary: sit tall, breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of four, pause, and breathe out gently for a count of six. Repeat for several rounds.
